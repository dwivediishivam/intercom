Create the complete product UI for a premium, modern customer communication platform used by support teams and their customers. The product should feel trustworthy, fast, collaborative, and highly polished across desktop, tablet, and mobile. The goal is to create an experience that stands out strongly among competing customer-support products.

Design every listed experience as a coherent system. Decide the visual language, layouts, information hierarchy, components, interactions, responsive behavior, animations, empty states, loading states, error states, and delightful details yourself. Explore useful original interactions, motion, micro-feedback, shortcuts, intelligent states, and other product ideas that improve usability and make the platform memorable. Add worthwhile product experience enhancements where they strengthen the result.

The application is multi-tenant: a business has a workspace, a team, customers, conversations, and a public help center. There are two internal team roles: Admin and Agent.

The product needs these screens and flows:

1. Authentication and onboarding
- Sign up and sign in with email/password and Google.
- Email verification, forgot/reset password, and authentication error states.
- First-time workspace creation and a concise onboarding experience.
- Invite teammates and assign Admin or Agent roles.

2. Main support inbox
- One unified inbox for live-chat and email conversations.
- Clear channel indicators, customer identity, conversation preview, assignee, SLA state, priority/context, and status.
- Filters for channel, assignee, and conversation status: Open, Snoozed, Resolved.
- Search and useful saved views if appropriate.
- Conversation assignment/reassignment, snoozing, resolving/reopening, and bulk-friendly flows if useful.
- Great empty states and an excellent responsive layout.

3. Conversation workspace
- A full conversation timeline supporting live chat and email threads in the same interface.
- Customer profile/context panel: full past-conversation history, timeline, pages visited, last seen, and useful details.
- Composer for agent replies, email replies, attachments, rich formatting if useful, send-state feedback, and message failure/retry states.
- Typing indicator, online/offline state, read receipts, and clear real-time feedback.
- Agent assignment and conversation controls.
- Canned responses: browse, search, tag, and insert saved replies quickly.
- AI issue summary: show what the customer wants, what has been tried, and the current status; support refreshing and show loading/fallback states.
- AI reply draft suggestions grounded in the conversation and relevant help articles, with simple controls for reviewing, editing, inserting, regenerating, or dismissing.
- SLA first-response/resolution indicators and breach states.

4. Customer-facing embedded chat widget
- A compact, friendly chat bubble that can be embedded on any website with one script tag.
- Welcome/open state, conversation state, returning-visitor history, typing, availability, read states, and message delivery feedback.
- Article suggestions appear naturally while a visitor writes a question.
- The widget must work beautifully in a small viewport and on mobile.
- Include a demo-page concept where a reviewer can use the installed widget.

5. Knowledge base management and public help center
- Internal management screens for categories, sections, article lists, article editor, drafts, publishing, and article status.
- A rich text article-writing experience.
- Public-facing help center with a home page, category/section browsing, article page, and powerful search.
- States for no results, loading, draft/private content, and helpful article feedback if appropriate.
- The public help center must also make sense when served on a connected custom domain.

6. Team and workspace settings
- Team member directory, invitations, role management, and safety confirmations.
- Workspace/brand settings.
- Support email configuration and health/status guidance.
- Widget installation page with copyable single-script-tag snippet and configuration controls.
- Custom knowledge-base domain configuration: domain entry, DNS records, verification/provisioning progress, TLS status, errors, and clear next actions.
- API key management and configurable outbound webhooks, including event selection and delivery/activity history.

7. Analytics and operations
- Analytics dashboard for response times, resolution rates, busiest hours, agent performance, volume by channel, and SLA health.
- Useful time ranges, comparison states, no-data state, and drill-down interactions where appropriate.
- Operational feedback for webhook/email/AI failures that helps a team act without overwhelming them.

8. Cross-product quality
- Define reusable design tokens and component patterns.
- Design accessibly: keyboard navigation, focus states, readable contrast, screen-reader-aware forms, and touch-friendly targets.
- Include loading, skeleton, success, warning, confirmation, permission-denied, offline, reconnecting, and error states throughout.
- Include practical notifications, undo opportunities for reversible actions, and appropriate confirmation for destructive actions.
- Make interaction and animation choices that enhance clarity and delight without slowing down support work.

Deliver a complete design system and all important product states, not just idealized happy-path screens. Treat this as a flagship product experience and introduce any additional high-value UX concepts that help the product feel exceptionally considered.
